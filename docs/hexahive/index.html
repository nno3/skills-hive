    Game Overview

    HexaHive is a puzzle game where you control hexagonal pieces and arrange them on a grid. The goal is to complete full rows of hexagons to clear them and earn points. As you progress, the game becomes faster and more challenging. Avoid filling the grid to the top, or the game will end!

    Controls

    • Arrow Left: Move the current piece to the left.
    • Arrow Right: Move the current piece to the right.
    • Arrow Down: Move the current piece down faster (soft drop).
    • Arrow Up: Rotate the current piece.
    • Space Bar: Instantly drop the piece to the bottom (hard drop).
    • P: Pause or resume the game.

    Scoring

    • Soft Drop: 1 point per step.
    • Hard Drop: 2 points per step.
    • Clearing Lines:
      • 1 line: 100 points × level
      • 2 lines: 300 points × level
      • 3 lines: 500 points × level
      • 4 lines: 800 points × level

    Tips and Strategies

    • Plan ahead and arrange pieces to clear multiple lines at once.
    • Use rotation to fit pieces into tight spaces.
    • Use the soft drop (Arrow Down) to position pieces accurately.
    • Use the hard drop (Space Bar) to quickly place pieces when you're sure of their position.
